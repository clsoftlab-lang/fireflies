# Code of Conduct

> Part of Fireflies · CC BY 4.0 · © 2026 CLSOFTLAB, Dr. Lee Il-guk

Fireflies is a place to make something quiet and beautiful together. How we treat each other is part
of the art.

한국어 요약: 이 프로젝트는 서로를 존중하는 곳입니다. 나이, 국적, 언어, 장애, 성별, 경험과 관계없이
모두를 환영하며, **특히 학생과 처음 오신 분을 환영합니다.** 괴롭힘과 차별은 허용하지 않습니다. 문제가
있으면 아래 연락처로 비공개로 알려 주십시오.

## Who this is for

Everyone is welcome, whatever their age, nationality, language, disability, body, ethnicity, religion,
gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, education, income, or experience.

**Students and young makers are welcome here as full participants.** Two of this project's contributors
are school students. A first question is not a bother; it is how the project grows. Nobody is expected
to already know how open source works.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good intent. Many people here write in a second or third language,
  and many are learning as they go.
- **Explain, don't gatekeep.** When someone is new, point them to the right doc, or open the door a
  little wider. "That's a good question" beats "you should already know this".
- **Critique ideas, not people.** Keep discussion about the work and the evidence.
- **Keep it safe.** Fireflies is gentle art. Do not push it toward any harmful use or anything the
  safety and leave-no-trace rules forbid. Take safety notes seriously and thank the person who raised one.
- **Respect privacy and consent.** Do not share other people's faces, names, exact locations or photos
  without clear permission — especially for minors.
- **Credit others' work.** Say where an idea, image or piece of code came from.

## What is not allowed

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention. This is a project many young people take part in;
  keep everything appropriate for them.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption, or pushing the project toward any harmful use.

## If something goes wrong

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, or have any concern, report it privately to the
project lead:

- Open a private report through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** channel (it is
  private and reaches the maintainers), or contact CLSOFTLAB / Dr. Lee Il-guk through the repository.
- You can write in English or Korean.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will listen, thank you, and respond. Depending on the situation,
outcomes range from a private word to a temporary or permanent ban from the project. People reporting
in good faith will be protected; retaliation is itself a violation.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project chat or
event — and when someone is representing the project in public.
